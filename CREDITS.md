## The 2021 prototype

Joly World was first a small Unity prototype by Trysac in 2021, uploaded as a draft to itch.io.
Its project files were lost; nothing from it is used here. The two captures in
[`docs/original`](docs/original) are screenshots Trysac took of that build. They may show
third-party art the prototype used (the character, item icons and textures), so they are kept
only as a record of the 2021 version and are not covered by this repository's MIT licence.
